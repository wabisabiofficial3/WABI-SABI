🌿 Sanctuary Privacy & Essential Cookies

We use strictly essential cookies and local browser storage to remember your audio ambiance, visual preferences, and bookclub navigation. Zero third-party trackers, zero advertising.

✦ Sanctuary Privacy & Data Promise

Our Philosophy: Wabi Sabi is created as an unhurried digital sanctuary. We believe reading and reflection should remain intimate and unmonitored.

  • Zero Surveillance: We do not run third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or commercial pixels.
  • Essential Local State: Sound ambience states (Rain sound), reflection margin notes, and theme choices remain purely inside your device's browser local storage.
  • Curator Sessions: Administrative accounts receive a secure, encrypted HTTP-only session cookie expiring after 30 days.
  • Community Presence: Any member details in the public directory are curated and managed transparently with direct consent.

Questions or requests to adjust your directory presence? Speak to any curator during our weekly salon or email us directly.
